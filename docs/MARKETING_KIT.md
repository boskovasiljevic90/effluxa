# Effluxa Launch Marketing Kit

## Core positioning

Effluxa is an AI financial intelligence product by NeedAIHelp. It helps SMEs, CFOs, accountants and consultants find financial leakage before it becomes profit loss.

## One-line description

Upload invoices, statements, CSV exports or Excel files and get an AI-powered view of overspending, duplicate-payment risk, vendor concentration, cash-flow pressure and practical savings opportunities.

## Primary promise

Find the financial leakage your reports are hiding — before it becomes profit loss.

## Who it is for

- SME owners who need a faster view of unnecessary spend.
- CFOs and finance leads who want a first-pass leakage screen.
- Accountants and consultants who need a repeatable client audit workflow.
- Agencies that want client workspaces, portfolio visibility and executive-ready reports.

## Launch FAQ answers

**Does Effluxa replace accounting software?**  
No. It is a financial leakage and decision-support layer, not bookkeeping, reconciliation, tax filing or payment approval software.

**What data do I need?**  
Any supported invoice, statement, expense export or spreadsheet that gives the audit useful financial context.

**Is the result financial advice?**  
No. It is AI-generated informational analysis and should be verified by a qualified professional before action.

**Can I cancel?**  
Yes. Pro and Agency subscriptions can be managed through Paddle’s customer portal from account settings.

**Who makes Effluxa?**  
Effluxa is a product by NeedAIHelp, an applied AI solutions company. VisaPilot is the next product in the portfolio.
